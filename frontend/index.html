VideoEditorY

Edit a video by editing its transcript.

1. Paste a YouTube link

The app downloads the video and transcribes it locally with Whisper — no manual caption download/upload needed. Pick the language that's actually spoken in the video for best accuracy.

2. Choose how to edit

Every cue Whisper produced, with its exact timestamp. Check the ones you want removed, then cut — no AI involved.

In Claude Desktop (or a claude terminal), attach/cd into this project folder. Click "Copy message for Claude" below, paste it as your first message, and send it — the message points Claude at AI_EDITING_INSTRUCTIONS.md so it reads the rules before doing anything. It'll read workspace/subtitles.vtt and write workspace/outline.md: the transcript broken into topic-based paragraphs with titles, correcting Whisper's transcription mistakes along the way.

Each paragraph/sub-paragraph below shows Claude's (possibly corrected) transcript text for that span — check the box on any paragraph or sub-paragraph to mark it for removal, then click "Cut selected sections".

Describe the overall result you want — e.g. "this is 40 minutes, trim it to about 15, keep only the important information." Claude decides what to remove. Click "Copy message for Claude" and paste it into Claude Desktop/terminal; it writes workspace/cuts.md for you.

Or paste a reply from another AI manually

Paste the full structured reply you got back from ChatGPT (or another AI) after giving it AI_EDITING_INSTRUCTIONS.md plus your instruction and transcript — including its ## REMOVED section.

3. Review cuts

Start End Dur. Text / reason

4. Choose audio & render

Keeps the original audio untouched, no voice-over, no music.

Generates a Piper voice-over for every surviving chunk automatically (no per-chunk review) and renders straight away. A real but minimal first version — use Manual TTS below if you want to review/fix chunks individually.

Text from everything your currently enabled cuts above would keep. If you used the AI outline edit mode, this is one chunk per outline paragraph/ sub-paragraph; otherwise it falls back to ~500-character chunks cut at cue boundaries. Click "Generate with Piper" on a chunk for a free, local, offline voice-over (no API key, runs on this machine), or copy the text into any other TTS tool and upload the result. Each chunk shows how many seconds of video it has to fill, and the check tells you if the audio is too long for that slot. The text in each chunk is editable — fix anything you want changed before copying or generating, since that text is exactly what gets read aloud. Once a chunk has audio (generated or uploaded), a player appears so you can listen right there.
